# Model cards (generated from the exported artifacts)

Generated by `scripts/build_model_cards.py`. Every number below is read from `backend/app/ml/artifacts/`.

> **The application's Model 1 and Model 2 are now the models of Smart_Car_Models.pdf: see [SPEC_MODELS.md](SPEC_MODELS.md).** This file documents the model trained on real inspections (the dashboard's comparison panel, section "Model 2" below) and the previous Model 1 (age + fuel), kept for reference and no longer used by the API.

## Model 1 - odometer estimate (kilometres driven) (v1.0.1, trained 2026-11-18)

* **Algorithm:** XGBoost regression, features age + fuel, monotone non-decreasing in age; q10/q90 XGBoost quantile models for the range.
* **Training data:** 113,858 cleaned used-car listings (7 UK used cars: 93,580, 8 India used cars: 7,196, 3 Cardekho: 13,082); UK odometers converted from miles to km; ages computed against each dataset's own snapshot year.
* **Held-out validation of the learned model (training markets, uncalibrated, 22,772 vehicles):** R2 = 0.551, RMSE = 23,387 km, MAE = 15,845 km; the raw 10-90% quantile range covers 81.5% of held-out vehicles (nominal 80%).
* **Calibration to Israel:** CBS Publication 1923 (Vehicle Kilometers Travelled 2022), Tables 4 and 8; fleet mean 15.2 (2022) / 14.7 (2023-24). Families fitted: age band, ownership; supported age 13 years (above it the official yearly rate replaces the flat learned curve). Registry sample used: 17,507 real vehicles.
* **Check on CBS Table 8 joint cells never used in the fit:** mean absolute error 3.6% over 8 cells.
* **Curve smoothing (final technical review):** centred moving average of the learned yearly increments, window 3 years (ages 1 to 13), total preserved; boosters not retrained.
* **Low-support fuels (hybrid, LPG/CNG) and low reliability:** fuel types with fewer than 30 training vehicles at an age continue with the yearly growth of the petrol curve above the last supported age {'hybrid': 7, 'lpg_cng': 7}; electric always uses the petrol curve; the API flags these estimates as low reliability.

**Interval:** indicative 80% range: quantile models scaled by calibrated/learned ratio; coverage of the raw range on held-out training-market vehicles is in held_out_validation_uncalibrated; NOT validated on Israeli vehicles.

**Limitations**

* no per-vehicle Israeli odometer data exist in open form: individual accuracy in Israel is unknown
* calibration fixes the level of population means (age band x ownership); it does not narrow the spread of individual vehicles
* assumes yearly usage is stationary across cohorts; electric vehicles are treated like petrol (CBS has no EV data); registry sample ages are as of 2026
* make/model are not used (no measurable value in experiments)

## Model 2 - risk that the next annual inspection finds a defect, by system (v1.0.0, trained 2026-11-18)

* **Target:** defect found at the NEXT annual inspection (about 12 months ahead) of the same vehicle, by system; NOT a breakdown and NOT a failure forecast.
* **Training data (primary):** UK DVSA MOT (OGL v3.0), 1-in-41 vehicle sample, test in 2022 -> next test in 2023: 660,202 pairs of 660,202 vehicles. **Validation:** Dutch RDW open data (public domain) (366,950 pairs).
* **Variants:** `cold` = age_year, gap_next, fuel, make_c; `cold_odo` = age_year, gap_next, fuel, make_c, km; `hist` = age_year, gap_next, fuel, make_c, cur_brakes, cur_electrical, cur_engine_emissions, cur_any_defect; `hist_odo` = age_year, gap_next, fuel, make_c, cur_brakes, cur_electrical, cur_engine_emissions, cur_any_defect, km.
* **Mileage estimated by Model 1 is not a feature:** not used: no gain in any experiment (notebooks 07, 09).
* **Coverage:** fuels petrol, diesel, hybrid; age at the last inspection 3-20 years; makes outside the list are mapped to OTHER (lower evidence).
* **Development split (UK):** train 278,210 / validation 59,910 / test 39,422 pairs; test pairs are later in time and from vehicles never seen in training.

## Model 2 - reference scale of the risk levels (v1.1.0, built 2026-11-19)

* **Reference population:** UK MOT pairs 2022 -> 2023, out-of-sample (3-fold cross-fitted by vehicle) scores, all ages 3-20, petrol/diesel/hybrid, interval to the next inspection standardised to 12 months.
* **Levels:** Low = lowest 36% of reference vehicles, Moderate = the next 33%, High = the top 31% (same cut points for the three systems).
* **How the cut points were chosen:** maximum log-likelihood of a three-level step function of the observed defects (UK cross-fitted, cold variant), each level holding at least 20% of the reference vehicles. Per-system optima: Brakes 39/76, Electrical 36/69, Engine-related 25/68; common optimum 36/69; share of the log-likelihood gain lost by using the common cut points: Brakes 1.8%, Electrical 0.0%, Engine-related 1.5%.
* **Same-age comparison (complementary):** below the 20th percentile of vehicles of the same age = lower; above the 80th percentile of vehicles of the same age = higher; otherwise typical.
